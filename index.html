RealityGap

Shipped public tool · Polymarket execution-quality radar

RealityGap scans live Polymarket markets and orderbooks to compare the visible price with the executable price for a selected size.

As a public case study, it is an example of AI-assisted product execution with live data, controlled validation, clear guardrails, and public deployment.

  • Executable entry price
  • Spread and orderbook depth
  • Slippage at selected size
  • Exit-liquidity risk

No wallet. No trading. No predictions. No financial advice. Execution quality only.
